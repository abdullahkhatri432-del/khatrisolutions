# Getting the first external links, honestly

The site has 0 external links and 23 pages sitting at "Discovered – currently not indexed". Google knows every URL; it just has no outside reason to crawl them. This file is the cheapest honest fix.

Nothing here buys links. Every item is a place where a real business can list real work.

## 2. LinkedIn (15 minutes, free)

- Add `https://khatrisolutions.vercel.app` under **Contact info → Website** on your personal profile.
- Create a free **Khatri Solutions** company page with the same URL, logo and one-line description.
- Post the studio site once from the company page. A post with a link is a real, crawled citation.

Also `nofollow`, but it is the single most common place a prospective client checks before emailing.

## 3. Republish the blog posts (30 minutes, free, real authority)

Dev.to and Hashnode both let you set a canonical URL, so the original stays on your domain and there is no duplicate-content problem.

- Take `how-much-does-a-website-cost-in-india` and any other post from `/blog`.
- Publish on [dev.to](https://dev.to) with `canonical_url` pointing at the full `khatrisolutions.vercel.app/blog/...` URL.
- Same post on [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com) if you want a second surface.

This is the one item on the list that produces genuine editorial links from high-authority domains rather than a profile field. It also gets your pricing content in front of people searching for exactly that.

## 4. Business listings (free, but only if the business is real)

| Where | What it gives | Catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Business Profile** | The strongest local signal in India. Appears in Maps and the local pack. | You must be a real business. A service-area business may hide its address, but verification is a real video/phone check and it can fail. **Never use an address you do not control.** |
| **Bing Places** | Feeds Bing and Copilot answers. | Free, lighter verification than Google. |
| **Apple Business Connect** | Apple Maps and Siri. | Free. |
| **GoodFirms** | Agency directory with portfolio pages. | Free tier only. Skip Clutch and DesignRush for now — they want client reviews and paid plans you do not have. |

Do these once you have a domain. Directory reviewers are noticeably stricter with a `*.vercel.app` URL, and the listing looks more credible with a real one.

---

## What not to do

- **Do not buy "500 directory submissions" or Fiverr/SEOClerks backlink packs.** They are the fastest route to a manual action and will undo everything above.
- **Do not do link exchanges** ("I link you, you link me"). Google discounts them and they are easy to detect at this scale.
- **Do not invent reviews or client logos** to qualify for Clutch, GoodFirms paid tiers or Google's review snippets. Your site's honesty about demos is an asset — keep it consistent everywhere.

---

## The one thing that would help most

A custom domain, roughly ₹1,000/year. `vercel.app` is a shared subdomain: it carries no brand trust, some directories reject it, and every citation you build points at a URL you do not own. Moving to a domain before you do the listings above means you build authority once instead of twice.
